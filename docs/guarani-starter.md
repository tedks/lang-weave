# Paraguayan Guaraní starter

Choose **Paraguayan Guaraní** in Settings. Beginner includes 23 words about nature, home, school and everyday reading. Expanded adds five more nature words for a total of 28. Use **Practice a paragraph** from the popup or Welcome to try your own English or load the built-in Guaraní example. Paragraph practice, My words and Review use the included vocabulary without a Gemini key. Weaving words into web pages uses the existing Gemini and site access settings.

The original nature words use the spellings and senses documented by the Dirección General de Investigación Lingüística at Paraguay's Secretaría de Políticas Lingüísticas. Its *Nombres propios en guaraní* explains the ordinary words behind names recorded in Paraguay. The starter pairs each word with a short English gloss written for Lang Weave. [Nombres propios en guaraní, Spanish edition](https://spl.gov.py/files/DGIL/Nombre_propio_%20guarani_ESP.pdf)

## Restoring a backup

Generated Guaraní packs keep their own selections and progress. For the bundled starter, choose the newest version required by anything in your backup.

| Your backup includes | Minimum Lang Weave version to restore |
| --- | --- |
| Original starter selection or nature-word progress | 0.12.26 |
| Reading-day dates (backup format 3) | 0.13.3 |
| Progress on a home or school word | 0.14.2 |
| Progress on an everyday prose word from the table above | 0.15.2 |

A backup containing reading days and a review of *aranduka* (book) requires 0.14.2 or later. A backup that includes a review of *okẽ* (door) requires 0.15.2 or later, even if it uses format 2. An older reader can refuse the entire restore when a card or reading history refers to a word it lacks. If the word appears only in the learning log, the reader reports those entries as omitted.
